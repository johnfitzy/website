I'm a backend software engineer at OpenSky Network, where I work on the systems that process and distribute global ADS-B aircraft surveillance data in real time.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of distributed, data-intensive systems and infrastructure: reactive microservices in Java and Quarkus, event streaming with Apache Kafka and Avro, Kubernetes deployments, and the occasional deep dive into something new. Most recently that's been Go.

I hold a Bachelor of Music majoring in Jazz Performance (guitar), a Graduate Diploma in Teaching and Learning, and a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science. For 14 years I played guitar in a dream pop band called French for Rabbits.

Outside of that I spend a lot of time in the water, surfing and sailing, and in the gym when the surf is flat.

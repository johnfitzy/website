---
title: "Kubernetes Home Lab"
description: "Building a three-node K3s cluster on old Lenovo mini PCs behind a MikroTik router."
---

A three-node K3s cluster built from old Lenovo ThinkCentre M910q mini PCs, on its own network behind a MikroTik hEX S router. I manage it from my laptop over WireGuard and provision it with Ansible.

The code is on GitHub: [kubernetes-cluster-ansible](https://github.com/johnfitzy/K3s-home-cluster).
